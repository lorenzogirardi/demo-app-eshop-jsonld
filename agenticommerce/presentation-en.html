The shifting landscapeHow customer expectations are changing

Natural language queries

Customers no longer search for "blue cotton t-shirt 42". They ask: "Something elegant for tonight, under €200". They expect commerce to understand.

Less patience for browsing

Filters, categories, manual comparison across dozens of pages: too much effort. Patience for traditional search is shrinking.

Expectation of guidance and personalization

Customers expect someone — or something — to guide the choice, explain options, and recommend based on context.

Commerce must stay relevant everywhere

Search increasingly starts from AI assistants. The brand must be findable, understandable, and chosen even in these paths.

La direzione del mercato aumenta l'aspettativa per esperienze conversazionali e assistite in ogni fase del journey.

Source: project documentation · market observations3

Il costo dell'inazioneWhat we risk if we don’t act

Diffuse experience Customer doesn’t know what to choose
→
Indecision Too much effort, too few answers
→
Abandonment Searches elsewhere or stops searching
→
Lower return Customer doesn’t come back
→
Competitive vulnerability Chooses a simpler competitor

Risk of lost conversion

Esperienze frammentate aumentano l'abbandono nella ricerca e nel carrello. Più passaggi e incertezza = più dropout.

Risk of lower retention

Se il cliente trova un'esperienza più semplice altrove, diminuisce la probabilità di ritorno. La fedeltà si guadagna con meno sforzo.

Rischio di minore frequenza d'acquisto

Scelte complesse si rimandano. Se l'esperienza non facilita la decisione, il cliente acquista meno spesso.

Risk of intermediation

AI agents increasingly mediate the customer’s choice. Without an Agentic presence, the brand may be excluded from the decision path.

Il costo potenziale dell'inazione è la combinazione di queste quattro dinamiche. Mantenere l'attuale base clienti richiede un'esperienza che riduca lo sforzo e aumenti la fiducia.

Che cos'è
Agentic Commerce

🔍 Discovery

Conversational search, guided product discovery

💬 Guidance

Motivated recommendations and comparisons

✅ Decision

Clear explanations, informed action

Non sostituisce necessariamente il sito tradizionale: lo potenzia. Il controllo resta sempre all'utente.

Casi d'uso che creano valoreDove l'Agentic Commerce fa la differenza

Generic request

Problem: "Something for tonight" doesn’t match any filter.

Experience: L'assistente chiede, interpreta, propone.

Benefit: Customer finds relevant options with less effort.

Technical need or constraints

Problem: Customer has specific needs but doesn’t know which filters to use.

Experience: Targeted questions, catalog-anchored answers.

Benefit: Informed choice, reduced risk of error.

Comparing alternatives

Problem: Decine di opzioni, nessuna spiegazione del perché sceglierne una.

Experience: Guided comparison with motivations for each choice.

Benefit: Greater confidence in the decision, less indecision.

Post-purchase

Problem: Repetitive questions about orders, returns, availability.

Experience: Assistant responding with catalog and knowledge base information.

Benefit: Reduced tickets, greater satisfaction.

Incomplete catalog content

Problem: Descriptions, attributes, and information inconsistent or hard to use.

Experience: AI enrichment and validation with human approval.

Benefit: Catalogo più completo e affidabile per l'assistente e per il cliente.

External AI agent access

Problem: Customer searches with ChatGPT or Perplexity and the brand is absent.

Experience: Catalog is readable and interactive for agents.

Benefit: Brand stays relevant even in new discovery channels.

Open source

MIT license. No proprietary dependencies. Active community and modular components.

Not a finished product

From user interaction to useful responseHow it works, in simple terms

1. Customer asks a question Expresses the need in their own words, in natural language
→
2. L'assistente interpreta Understands context and asks for clarification when needed
→
3. Uses catalog and content Searches the company catalog and proposes relevant options
→
4. Risposta spiegata e orientata all'azione Customer receives comparison, motivation, and can act

Fundamental principle

Nessuna azione commerciale rilevante senza conferma dell'utente. L'assistente propone e spiega; il cliente decide.

Perché è diverso da un chatbot

It doesn’t generate generic responses: it’s anchored to the real catalog, real prices, and company rules. Every recommendation is motivated and verifiable.

Defending the customer base

The benefit isn’t just acquisition. It reduces the reasons existing customers leave, improves self-service, and makes the brand easier to choose.

Without this evolution, we could see a gradual migration toward simpler experiences offered by competitors or AI agents.

Growth lever

Customers who couldn’t find the right product can now do so. Large and technical catalogs become accessible. New conversational channels expand reach.

It’s a strategic thesis to validate with internal KPIs: we’re not presenting proven results, but a measurable path.
